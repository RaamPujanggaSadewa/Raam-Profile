# AI-Assisted Workflow — Raam Pujangga Sadewa

AI is an active part of my design practice, not a background tool. I use it across the full product lifecycle — from discovery through documentation.

---

## How I Use AI

### Research Planning & Synthesis
Structuring user research study plans, synthesizing findings from user interviews, and processing usability session outputs into actionable insights faster and with greater consistency.

### Rapid Prototyping Support
Exploring layout and interaction variants by iterating with AI-assisted generation, reducing the time from idea to testable concept.

### Prompt-Driven UX Exploration
Using prompts to pressure-test design decisions, surface edge cases, and explore alternative user flows before committing to a direction.

### Accelerated Insight Generation & Design Recommendation
Turning raw research data into prioritized findings and design directions — compressing synthesis cycles without sacrificing depth.

---

## Where This Has Been Applied

At FLIK, AI-assisted workflows are integrated across:

- **Product discovery** — faster synthesis of user feedback and behavioral data
- **Requirement definition** — PRD drafting and structuring with Claude Code + GitHub
- **Design exploration** — evaluating and pressure-testing multiple design directions
- **Documentation** — maintaining consistency across specs and handoff materials
